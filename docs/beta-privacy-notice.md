# WPMark Beta Privacy Notice

Version 1.0 · Effective 25 September 2026

This notice explains how we handle your personal data when you sign up for,
and take part in, the WPMark beta programme. It is separate from the WPMark
plugin itself: **the plugin runs on your website and sends us nothing.** For
how the plugin handles your site's data, see
[privacy and your data](privacy-and-data.md).

## Who is responsible

**Atul Singh**, an individual based in India, trading as atulsingh.co.in,
is responsible for your data (the "controller" or "data fiduciary").
Contact for privacy questions, requests and grievances: **join@atulsingh.co.in**.

## What we collect

| Data | Source |
|---|---|
| Name, work email, company name, job role, country | The sign-up form |
| Website address, form and SEO plugins used, what you hope to use WPMark for (optional) | The sign-up form |
| Your consent to the beta terms, and the date you gave it | The sign-up form |
| Messages and feedback you send us, including health reports you choose to paste | Email, the beta channel, GitHub |
| Basic email engagement (for example whether an email was delivered) | Our email service |

We don't ask for, and you shouldn't send us, passwords, connection keys or
your website visitors' data.

## Why we use it, and on what basis

| Purpose | Legal basis |
|---|---|
| Run the beta: send the download, setup guide, updates and security notices | Your consent, and taking steps you asked for |
| Answer your questions and help you set up | Your request, and our legitimate interest in supporting users |
| Improve WPMark using your feedback | Our legitimate interest in improving the product |
| Contact you about related services, only if you ticked the box for this | Your consent (you can withdraw it at any time) |
| Keep a record of consent and meet legal duties | Legal obligation, and our legitimate interest |

We don't sell your data, use it for advertising profiles, or make automated
decisions about you with legal or similarly significant effects.

## Who we share it with

Only service providers that help us run the programme, under contract. These
are our **website host**, our **email service**, and **GitHub** (if you
choose to use GitHub issues, which are public). They may process data outside
your country, including in India, the EU or the US. Where required, we use
appropriate safeguards such as standard contractual clauses. We may also
disclose data if the law requires it.

## How long we keep it

While the beta programme runs and for **12 months** after it ends or after
you leave, whichever is sooner. Then we delete it, unless the law requires us
to keep something longer, such as a record of your consent. You can ask us to
delete it sooner.

## Your rights

Depending on where you live, you can:

- access your data, or get a copy;
- correct or update it;
- delete it;
- withdraw consent at any time (this doesn't affect processing before you
  withdrew);
- object to or restrict processing based on legitimate interests;
- ask for your data in a portable format;
- nominate someone to exercise your rights if you can't (India); and
- complain to a data protection authority. For example: the Data Protection
  Board of India, your EU or UK supervisory authority, or the authority in
  your UAE emirate or free zone, Saudi Arabia (SDAIA) or other GCC country.

To exercise any right, email **join@atulsingh.co.in**. We'll reply within 30
days, or sooner where the law requires. Please contact us first for
grievances; we'll try to resolve them quickly.

California and other US state residents: we don't sell or share personal
information for cross-context behavioural advertising. You have the rights
above, and we won't treat you differently for using them.

## Security

We use reasonable technical and organisational measures to protect your
data, such as access controls, encrypted connections and two-factor
authentication on our accounts. No system is completely secure. If a breach
affects your data, we will tell you and the authorities where the law
requires it.

## Children

The beta programme is for business users aged 18 and over. We don't
knowingly collect data from children.

## Changes

We'll post updates here with a new date, and email beta members about
material changes.
