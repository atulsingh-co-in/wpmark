# Getting help

WPMark is free and open source, made and maintained by one person. Here is
how to get help, and what is available beyond the free plugin.

## Free help

1. **The guides:** [getting started](docs/getting-started.md),
   [what you can ask](docs/what-you-can-ask.md) and
   [troubleshooting](docs/troubleshooting.md).
2. **The health check:** **WPMark → Health check** finds most connection
   problems and says how to fix them.
3. **GitHub issues:** for bugs and ideas,
   [open an issue](https://github.com/atulsingh-co-in/wpmark/issues/new/choose).
   Include the health report. Never include passwords, keys or visitor data.
4. **Beta members** also get the beta channel shared when you join.

Free help is best effort, with no guaranteed response time. Priority goes to
bugs that affect many sites and to security issues.

Free help doesn't cover: setting up your hosting, firewall, caching or
security plugins; fixing other plugins; or building new features for one site.

Write to **join@atulsingh.co.in** with a few lines about your site and what
